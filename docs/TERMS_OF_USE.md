## 2. Religious content: please read

- **Quran:** the Arabic text comes from the verified Tanzil Project and is never altered by the app. **Translations and tafsir** are human works by named scholars and translators. They convey meaning and are not the Quran itself.
- **Hadith:** shown as published in the classical collections, with the gradings recorded by named scholars. **Not every hadith in every collection is authentic (sahih).** Check the grade shown on each hadith. The app doesn't issue its own gradings. Some translations in the hadith dataset have no named translator, and the app labels these. For scholarly purposes, check them against a printed edition.
- **Ask Islam (AI):** answers are AI-generated reflections based on retrieved Quran verses. **They are not fatwas, rulings or religious advice.** They may be incomplete or wrong. For any matter of fiqh, consult a qualified scholar.
- **Prayer times, Qibla, Hijri dates and Zakat** are calculated estimates. Their accuracy depends on your location, chosen method, device sensors and assumptions. Follow your local mosque or authority where it differs. Compass readings can be affected by nearby metal and magnets.

If you find an error, please use the **Report** button. Content corrections take priority over everything else.
